Learn the Game

Baseball combines individual skill with teamwork, strategy, and quick decisions.

Nine Innings

A standard professional game has nine innings, although extra innings may be needed to break a tie.

Positions

Teams use nine defensive positions, including pitcher, catcher, infielders, and outfielders.

Three Outs

Each half-inning ends when the defense records three outs.
